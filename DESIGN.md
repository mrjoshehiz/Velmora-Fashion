# Velmora visual direction

Keep the existing warm paper surfaces, wine accent, serif display typography and fashion photography. Product discovery and shopping are the primary goals.

- Let imagery and garment names carry the hierarchy. No decorative badges, online-status claims, section counters or repeating marquees.
- Still, responsive product grids replace automatic scrolling. Keep product links, size selection, saved items and bag controls.
- Use generous spacing between sections and compact spacing within product captions. Shared page gutters range from 20px to 80px.
- Homepage headings use Georgia at 36–96px with tracking no tighter than -0.035em. Preserve the incumbent body typography during this refinement; no external font request is added.
- Keep text below hero photographs on the paper surface, rather than on gradient overlays.
- Primary navigation prioritizes shopping. Secondary experiences remain available through Explore, the mobile menu and the footer.
- Show approved customer reviews when available; omit illustrative testimonials from the shopping homepage.
- Use visible keyboard focus, 44px shopping controls and reduced-motion support. The shared Radix Sheet manages mobile menu focus and Escape dismissal.

Implementation lives in `app/editorial.css`, loaded after the incumbent shared stylesheet. Existing service, account, product and administrative layouts retain their functional structure.
